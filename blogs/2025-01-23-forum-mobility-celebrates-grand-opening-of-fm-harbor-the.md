Forum Mobility officially opened FM Harbor, our flagship charging depot located at the Port of Long Beach. With 44 high-speed chargers delivering 9 megawatts of power, FM Harbor is the largest heavy-duty electric truck charging facility at any U.S. port—capable of supporting over 200 trucks per day.
