Announcing FM Santa Fe: ultra-fast charging, ultra-convenient location. This heavy-duty charging depot will feature an awesome 14 MCS pull-through lanes — allowing for a recharge in as little as 30 minutes — and 6 CCS bobtail ports. It’s optimized for all OEMs.
